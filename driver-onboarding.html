1. Parties

This agreement is between CAR 4 U 1 LTD ("the Company") and the driver named in this application ("the Driver").

2. Vehicle Hire

The Company agrees to provide the Driver with a vehicle for private hire work, subject to availability, licensing, insurance requirements and the terms of this agreement.

The specific vehicle registration, mileage, rental start date and any other vehicle details may be recorded by the Company when a vehicle is assigned to the Driver.

3. Rental Payments

The Driver agrees to pay the agreed vehicle rental charge at the frequency and amount agreed with CAR 4 U 1 LTD.

Rental payments must be made on time. Any unpaid rental, charges or other sums due may be recorded as an outstanding balance on the Driver's account.

4. Deposit

Where a deposit is required, the Driver agrees to pay the amount requested by the Company.

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of the rental, the deposit may be applied towards sums properly due under this agreement, including unpaid rental or damage for which the Driver is responsible.

5. Vehicle Condition

The Driver must take reasonable care of the vehicle and keep it in a clean and roadworthy condition.

The Driver must promptly report any warning lights, mechanical problems, damage, tyre problems or other safety-related concerns to CAR 4 U 1 LTD.

6. Daily Checks

The Driver is responsible for carrying out reasonable routine checks before using the vehicle, including tyres, lights, fluid levels where appropriate and any obvious safety defects.

7. Authorised Driver

Only a person authorised by CAR 4 U 1 LTD and properly covered by the relevant insurance and licensing requirements may drive the vehicle.

The Driver must not allow another person to drive or use the vehicle without prior permission from the Company and confirmation that all necessary insurance and licensing requirements have been satisfied.

8. Private Hire Use

Where the vehicle is supplied for private hire work, the Driver must hold all licences, badges and permissions required by the relevant licensing authority and comply with the applicable private hire conditions.

The Driver must immediately tell CAR 4 U 1 LTD if their driving licence, taxi/private hire licence or badge is suspended, revoked, expired or otherwise restricted.

9. Insurance

The Driver must comply with all conditions of the applicable motor insurance policy.

The Driver must immediately inform the Company of any change that could affect insurance cover, including penalty points, driving convictions, accidents, licence restrictions or other relevant changes.

10. Driving Behaviour

The Driver must operate the vehicle safely and responsibly and comply with road traffic law.

Repeated dangerous driving behaviour, excessive speeding, harsh braking, aggressive acceleration or other conduct that creates an unacceptable insurance or safety risk may result in action being taken by the Company, including withdrawal of the vehicle where appropriate.

11. Accidents and Damage

Any accident, collision, theft, vandalism or damage involving the vehicle must be reported to CAR 4 U 1 LTD as soon as reasonably possible.

The Driver must provide accurate details and cooperate with reasonable requests relating to the incident, insurance claim or vehicle repair.

12. Insurance Excess

Where an insurance excess or other charge is properly payable by the Driver following an accident or incident, the Driver agrees to pay the applicable amount subject to the insurance policy, this agreement and applicable law.

13. Fines and Penalties

The Driver is responsible for fines, penalties, parking charges, toll charges and similar charges arising from their use of the vehicle where the law permits those charges to be passed to the Driver.

The Company may provide the Driver's details to the relevant authority where legally required or permitted.

14. Fuel

Unless otherwise agreed, the Driver is responsible for fuel used during the rental period and must use the correct fuel for the vehicle.

15. Smoking

Smoking or vaping inside the vehicle is not permitted where prohibited by law, licensing conditions or Company policy.

16. Illegal Use

The vehicle must not be used for any unlawful purpose, racing, deliberate misuse or activity that would invalidate insurance or breach licensing requirements.

17. Maintenance and Repairs

The Driver must not arrange substantial repairs, modifications or alterations to the vehicle without prior authorisation from CAR 4 U 1 LTD, except where immediate action is reasonably necessary for safety.

18. Servicing, MOT and Inspections

The Driver must make the vehicle available when reasonably requested for servicing, MOT testing, licensing inspections, repairs, tyre replacement, recalls or other necessary work.

19. Vehicle Documents

Documents supplied by the Company relating to the vehicle, insurance or private hire licensing must be kept secure and used only for their intended purpose.

20. Change of Circumstances

The Driver must promptly notify CAR 4 U 1 LTD of material changes to their contact details, home address, driving licence, penalty points, taxi/private hire badge, licensing status or insurance-related circumstances.

21. Return of Vehicle

When the rental ends, the Driver must return the vehicle, keys and any Company property or vehicle documents in accordance with arrangements made with CAR 4 U 1 LTD.

The vehicle should be returned in reasonable condition, allowing for fair wear and tear.

22. Ending the Rental

Either party may end the rental in accordance with any notice period separately agreed between the parties and subject to applicable law.

Nothing in this agreement removes any statutory rights or obligations that cannot legally be excluded.

23. Serious Breach

The Company may require the vehicle to be returned immediately where reasonably necessary because of a serious breach, loss of insurance or licensing eligibility, unlawful use, serious safety concerns or other circumstances making continued use unlawful or unsafe.

24. Information Provided by Driver

The Driver confirms that the information provided in this application is true and accurate to the best of their knowledge.

The Driver understands that materially false or misleading information may affect whether CAR 4 U 1 LTD is able to provide or continue providing a vehicle.

25. Electronic Documents

The Driver agrees that documents relating to the rental may be provided electronically, including by email, secure web link or messaging service where appropriate.

26. Electronic Signature

By signing below and selecting the acceptance checkbox, the Driver intends to sign this agreement electronically and confirms their agreement to its terms.

27. Data and Records

CAR 4 U 1 LTD may retain information and documents reasonably required to administer the rental, licensing, insurance, compliance and related business records, subject to applicable data protection law.

28. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by the law of Scotland, and disputes will be subject to the jurisdiction of the Scottish courts where applicable.

29. Entire Agreement

This document, together with any vehicle-specific rental details subsequently recorded and any other terms expressly agreed between the parties, forms the agreement relating to the vehicle rental.

30. Driver Confirmation

By signing below, the Driver confirms that they have had the opportunity to read this agreement, understand its contents and agree to be bound by its terms.
